StudX Returns, Refunds & Disputes Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

This Policy explains cancellations, returns, refunds, and disputes for StudX transactions.

It supplements the Horizon Synergy Terms of Service, Horizon Synergy Privacy Policy, Horizon Synergy Return Policy, and other applicable StudX policies.

For StudX marketplace transactions, this Policy generally takes precedence over the general Horizon Synergy Return Policy where both address the same marketplace transaction.

StudX marketplace framework

This policy forms part of the StudX marketplace framework. StudX is a Horizon Synergy venture, and StudX-specific policies supplement the Horizon Synergy Terms of Service and Horizon Synergy Privacy Policy.

For StudX, the applicable framework is the Horizon Synergy Terms of Service + Horizon Synergy Privacy Policy + StudX-specific policies.

StudX Legal

Acceptable Use Seller Policy Shipping & Fulfilment Returns, Refunds & Disputes

1. Marketplace Role

StudX provides technology and marketplace infrastructure for discovery, communication, transactions, and fulfilment arrangements.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, StudX is not the manufacturer, owner, seller, service provider, food provider, or fulfilment provider for independent sellers.

Sellers remain responsible for goods/services.

2. Buyer Cancellation

Buyer may generally cancel before seller acceptance.

After acceptance, cancellation may still be possible depending on circumstances.

A cancellation fee may apply after seller acceptance.

The cancellation fee is percentage-based and subject to applicable StudX rules.

3. Cancellation After Handover

After handover, cancellation may generally be declined unless:

  • Item is returned to seller; or
  • Another valid refund/dispute basis exists.

Consider:

  • Item condition.
  • Whether used.
  • Original condition.
  • Circumstances.
  • Evidence.

Cancellation-related fee may still apply where permitted.

4. Seller Cancellation

If seller cancels an accepted transaction without an appropriate reason, StudX may:

  • Cancel transaction.
  • Refund buyer.
  • Warn seller.
  • Apply cancellation fee.
  • Restrict account.
  • Suspend/terminate seller for excessive/serious cancellations.

5. Non-Delivery

Buyer may request refund where accepted order is not received.

StudX may review information from both parties.

Where seller cannot provide appropriate explanation/supporting evidence, StudX may cancel and refund.

6. Incorrect or Incomplete Orders

Seller may receive reasonable opportunity to correct issue.

Correction period may be determined by buyer/seller agreement or StudX based on circumstances.

If issue cannot reasonably be corrected, StudX may cancel and refund.

7. Damaged or Defective Goods

StudX may request evidence from both parties, including:

  • Before-handover photos.
  • After-handover photos.
  • Platform messages.
  • Seller explanation.
  • Buyer explanation.
  • Other relevant evidence.

StudX may refund where evidence supports buyer.

Refund may be declined where evidence indicates buyer misuse, post-handover damage, fraud, or other circumstances for which seller should not reasonably be responsible.

8. Items Not as Described

Buyer may report material differences between listing and actual item/service.

Examples:

  • Materially inaccurate condition.
  • Misleading photographs.
  • Incorrect specifications.
  • Materially different product.
  • Service substantially different from advertised.

Seller may be given opportunity to resolve.

Where seller materially misrepresented listing, StudX may:

  • Cancel.
  • Refund buyer.
  • Restrict listing.
  • Apply additional percentage-based deduction/fee where permitted.
  • Restrict/suspend seller.

The exact percentage is not fixed in this policy and may depend on applicable StudX rules and circumstances.

9. Dispute Resolution

Buyers and sellers are encouraged to resolve ordinary issues between themselves where reasonably possible.

StudX may provide communication tools.

Either party may request StudX intervention.

Evidence may include:

  • In-platform messages.
  • Photos.
  • Order records.
  • Payment records.
  • Fulfilment confirmations.
  • Seller information.
  • Buyer information.
  • Other relevant evidence.

StudX may determine an outcome based on reasonably available information.

10. Fraud and Abuse

StudX may decline refunds/remedies where there is evidence of:

  • Fraudulent claims.
  • Deliberate damage.
  • False evidence.
  • Payment fraud.
  • Refund-system abuse.
  • Collusion.
  • Manipulated confirmations.

Fraudulent activity may result in suspension/termination and referral to appropriate authorities where appropriate.

11. Refunds and Commission

Where refund approved, StudX may reverse/adjust applicable transaction amounts, including commission, depending on reason and payment-provider processes.

Treatment may vary based on:

  • Transaction stage.
  • Whether seller received funds.
  • Refund reason.
  • Payment-provider limitations.
  • Applicable fees.

StudX will not intentionally create a refund process that gives a user more than they were entitled to receive from the original transaction, except where required by law or expressly authorized.

12. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are generally returned through original payment method.

Timing may depend on payment provider and financial institution.

StudX cannot guarantee exact refund timing.

13. Seller Responsibility

Seller remains responsible for resolving legitimate issues concerning their goods/services.

StudX may facilitate disputes but does not guarantee an outcome for either party.

14. Food Transactions

StudX Eats vendors remain responsible for food/services.

Refund decisions may consider:

  • Incorrect orders.
  • Missing items.
  • Serious quality issues.
  • Vendor cancellation.
  • Non-fulfilment.
  • Other legitimate circumstances.

Food complaints may need to be reported promptly because later verification can be difficult.

Repeated/serious food safety complaints may result in investigation, suspension, or termination.

15. Services

Service refunds may consider:

  • Whether service was provided.
  • Whether it matched listing.
  • Cancellation circumstances.
  • Seller failure to attend/deliver.
  • Whether buyer received agreed service.
  • Evidence from both parties.

Where service has already been substantially performed, refund may not be appropriate unless another valid basis exists.
