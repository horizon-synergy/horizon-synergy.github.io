StudX Seller Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

This Seller Policy establishes requirements and responsibilities for users who sell goods or services through StudX.

It supplements the Horizon Synergy Terms of Service and StudX Acceptable Use Policy.

StudX marketplace framework

This policy forms part of the StudX marketplace framework. StudX is a Horizon Synergy venture, and StudX-specific policies supplement the Horizon Synergy Terms of Service and Horizon Synergy Privacy Policy.

For StudX, the applicable framework is the Horizon Synergy Terms of Service + Horizon Synergy Privacy Policy + StudX-specific policies.

StudX Legal

Acceptable Use Seller Policy Shipping & Fulfilment Returns, Refunds & Disputes

1. Seller Eligibility

StudX is primarily intended for students.

Students from different educational institutions may participate if they meet eligibility and verification requirements.

StudX may permit limited access to non-student users, including viewing or other functionality, without necessarily allowing those users to operate as sellers.

StudX may expand eligibility or introduce additional communities in the future.

2. Seller Verification

StudX may use multiple methods to verify student eligibility and seller identity.

Verification may include:

  • Institutional student email verification.
  • Student cards.
  • Proof of registration.
  • Other student-status evidence.
  • Identity documentation.
  • Banking information.
  • Bank-account verification.
  • Other information required by StudX or payment providers.

Possessing a student email address is not necessarily required where alternative documentation can establish student status.

Sellers must provide accurate information and cooperate with reasonable verification requests.

3. Payout Eligibility

Sellers must complete the verification process required by StudX and its payment providers before receiving marketplace payouts.

StudX may require additional information or verification where necessary to comply with payment-provider requirements, prevent fraud, or satisfy legal/regulatory obligations.

StudX may delay or restrict payouts where verification is incomplete or where a transaction is subject to a legitimate dispute, refund, investigation, or other restriction.

4. Seller Responsibilities

Sellers are responsible for:

  • Accurate goods/services descriptions.
  • Truthful photographs and information.
  • Accurate pricing.
  • Availability.
  • Responding to orders within a reasonable period.
  • Accepting or declining orders accurately.
  • Fulfilling accepted orders.
  • Appropriate buyer communication.
  • Legal compliance.
  • StudX policy compliance.
  • Providing goods/services that reasonably correspond to listings.

Sellers are responsible for legality, safety, quality, and suitability of goods/services unless expressly stated otherwise.

5. Services

Sellers may offer lawful services such as tutoring, personal services, creative services, and other legitimate student-oriented services.

Sellers offering services are responsible for:

  • Clearly describing services.
  • Stating relevant limitations/requirements.
  • Delivering the agreed service.
  • Maintaining qualifications where legally required.
  • Complying with professional, safety, and regulatory requirements.

StudX does not automatically verify professional qualifications unless specifically stated.

6. Orders

A buyer may purchase directly, use the cart, or communicate with the seller where available.

Payment does not necessarily mean an order has been accepted.

The seller may accept or decline according to the platform transaction process.

Once accepted, the seller is expected to fulfil the order unless circumstances prevent fulfilment.

7. Cancellations

A seller may cancel where appropriate.

Repeated, excessive, or unjustified cancellations may result in:

  • Warnings.
  • Cancellation fees.
  • Reduced platform privileges.
  • Temporary suspension.
  • Permanent account termination.

Where a seller cancels an accepted order, the buyer may generally be entitled to a refund under the StudX Returns, Refunds & Disputes Policy.

8. StudX Commission

StudX may charge sellers a commission on completed transactions.

The applicable commission is disclosed to sellers.

Current standard commission: 10%.

This may change where permitted by applicable terms and platform disclosures.

Additional fees/deductions may apply in circumstances such as materially misleading listings, cancellations, disputes, or policy violations where permitted.

9. Seller Payouts

After a transaction is completed or otherwise becomes eligible for settlement, the seller may receive applicable proceeds after StudX commission and other applicable deductions.

Buyer confirmation may make a transaction eligible for payout, provided:

  • Required seller verification is complete.
  • No valid dispute/refund restriction applies.
  • The transaction satisfies payment-provider requirements.

StudX may use third-party payment providers.

Settlement timing may depend on the payment provider.

10. Seller Account Restrictions

StudX may restrict/suspend sellers for:

  • Fraud.
  • Repeated failed fulfilment.
  • Repeated cancellations.
  • Materially misleading listings.
  • Prohibited goods/services.
  • Unsafe conduct.
  • Abuse/harassment.
  • Payment manipulation.
  • Repeated buyer complaints.
  • Attempts to circumvent platform controls.

Serious violations may result in immediate termination.
