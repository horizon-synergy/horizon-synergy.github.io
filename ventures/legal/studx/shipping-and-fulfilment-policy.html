StudX Shipping & Fulfilment Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

This Policy explains fulfilment and delivery responsibilities on StudX.

StudX is a marketplace and technology platform. It does not currently operate as a fulfilment centre or automatically take physical possession of goods sold through the marketplace.

StudX marketplace framework

This policy forms part of the StudX marketplace framework. StudX is a Horizon Synergy venture, and StudX-specific policies supplement the Horizon Synergy Terms of Service and Horizon Synergy Privacy Policy.

For StudX, the applicable framework is the Horizon Synergy Terms of Service + Horizon Synergy Privacy Policy + StudX-specific policies.

StudX Legal

Acceptable Use Seller Policy Shipping & Fulfilment Returns, Refunds & Disputes

1. Seller Fulfilment

Unless a specific StudX service states otherwise, sellers are responsible for fulfilling orders.

Sellers and buyers may arrange:

  • In-person collection.
  • Campus meetups.
  • Seller-arranged delivery.
  • Third-party delivery services.
  • Other mutually agreed arrangements.

StudX does not guarantee availability, safety, quality, or performance of third-party delivery services.

2. Buyer and Seller Coordination

After order acceptance, buyer and seller coordinate practical fulfilment details including:

  • Meeting location.
  • Collection time.
  • Delivery arrangements.
  • Contact information.
  • Special requirements.

Users should exercise appropriate caution when arranging physical meetups.

3. Proof of Fulfilment

StudX may use transaction confirmations and other evidence to determine whether an order was fulfilled.

Where supported, both parties may confirm that an item/service was successfully handed over/completed.

Buyer confirmation may be sufficient to mark a transaction completed where appropriate.

StudX may introduce additional verification methods, including photographic/image-based verification, in the future.

4. Non-Delivery

If an accepted order is not fulfilled, the buyer may report the transaction and request a refund.

StudX may investigate using:

  • Platform messages.
  • Order information.
  • Photographs.
  • Seller explanations.
  • Buyer explanations.
  • Confirmation records.
  • Other relevant evidence.

Where appropriate, the transaction may be cancelled and buyer refunded.

5. Delivery Responsibility

Unless StudX explicitly provides delivery, StudX is not responsible for physically transporting goods.

Seller remains responsible for fulfilling orders according to listings and agreements.

Independent courier/delivery providers may have separate terms.

6. StudX Eats

Food vendors remain responsible for:

  • Food preparation.
  • Food quality.
  • Food safety.
  • Ingredients/allergens.
  • Order accuracy.
  • Packaging.
  • Fulfilment.
  • Applicable legal/regulatory compliance.

StudX provides marketplace/ordering infrastructure and does not generally prepare, manufacture, or physically handle food sold through StudX Eats.

StudX may investigate food quality, safety, inaccurate orders, repeated delays, or other issues and may restrict/suspend vendors where appropriate.

7. Services

For services, fulfilment means the service was provided according to the agreed arrangement.

Seller is responsible for:

  • Attending/delivering as agreed.
  • Communicating changes/cancellations.
  • Meeting legal/professional requirements.
  • Providing the service substantially as described.
